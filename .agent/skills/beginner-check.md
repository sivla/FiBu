# Skill: beginner-check

Use this skill to improve beginner clarity.

Check that the text answers:

- What do I see in Business Central?
- What do I click or enter?
- Why is the step needed?
- Which setup/master data is behind it?
- What happens if it is wrong?
- How do I correct or stop safely?
- How do I recognize success?
- Is this labor evidence or final proof?
