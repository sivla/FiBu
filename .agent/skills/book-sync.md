# Skill: book-sync

Use this skill when updating the book.

## Rules

- Change only evidence-backed statements.
- Keep German terms leading; add English terms only as UI/search help.
- Mark CRONUS-USA as labor, not German final proof.
- Do not claim German 19 percent VAT, German chart of accounts or compliance finality without evidence.
- Explain what a beginner sees, does, why it matters, what can go wrong and how to verify the result.
- Screenshots must be explained by purpose, not merely inserted.
